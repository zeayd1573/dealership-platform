Finance applications

If you apply for finance, the application is handled by a regulated finance provider on their own system, not on this website. We do not collect or store your PPS number, date of birth, income, employment history or bank details here. The provider will give you their own privacy notice, and they will explain any credit searches they carry out.

Who we share it with

  • Our website and hosting provider, who run this site for us and can access what is stored on it.
  • Finance providers, where you have asked us to arrange finance.
  • Anyone we are legally required to tell, such as An Garda Síochána or the Revenue Commissioners.

We do not sell your data, and we do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a sale: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Records relating to a sale: as long as we are required to keep them for tax and consumer law, normally six years.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months, and only if you agreed to it.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device. We set as few as we can. Nothing that tracks you runs until you say yes, and you can change your answer at any time using cookie settings.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete it, where we do not need to keep it
  • restrict or object to what we are doing with it
  • send it to you, or to someone else, in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent, where we relied on it, which does not affect anything done beforehand

Ask us using the contact details above and we will reply within one month. There is no charge.

Complaints

If you are not happy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, at dataprotection.ie or on 01 765 0100.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top. If the change is significant we will ask for your cookie choices again.
